How it works

🤖

Self-managing AI pipeline

A scheduler wakes every day, discovers free AI models across providers, auto-switches between them and collects until the daily target is met.

📑

Deep, structured briefings

Every item is a 500+ word deep-dive: background, analysis sections, key facts, implications and outlook — enforced by schema.

🔗

Linked & graph-ready

Entities, related items and taxonomy are cross-linked. A 500+ node knowledge graph is rebuilt automatically every day.

🧾

Full provenance

Each record carries its source, generating model, verification score and review status — a traceable machine dataset.
