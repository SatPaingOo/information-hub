## Background

SurgePix has emerged as a notable product on Product Hunt, showcasing AI visuals with a distinctive agent-callable editing capability. ## Agentic AI and Layer-Level Control

The core innovation of SurgePix lies in its agent-callable architecture, which enables users to interact with AI visual generation at a granular layer level. Unlike traditional AI image generators that produce static outputs, SurgePix's system treats each visual element as an editable node that can be manipulated through intelligent agents. This creates a powerful workflow where creators can specify exact modifications to specific layers—such as adjusting color grading, compositing elements, or modifying lighting—without rebuilding the entire image from scratch. The agent-callable interface essentially acts as a middleware that understands the hierarchical structure of AI-generated imagery, allowing for sophisticated, iterative refinement that mimics professional post-production techniques.
