## Disabled people in England to get 24-hour free bus travel

- **Type**: digest · **Topic**: politics · **Region**: global
- **Source**: [feeds.bbci.co.uk](https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/politics/rss.xml)
- **Note**: [[info:item:politics:global:2026-08-18-005]]
- **TL;DR**: From April, disabled people in England will be able to use their free bus passes at any time of day, removing previous weekday time restrictions. The £60m scheme, funded by the Department for Transport and Department for Work and Pensions, is expected to benefit around one million people and aligns with broader cost-of-living and employment initiatives.
