# Daily Hub — 2026-11-16

## Five EU countries announce migrant deportation centre in Rwanda

- **Type**: digest · **Topic**: world · **Region**: global
- **Source**: [feeds.bbci.co.uk](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw987343352go?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
- **Note**: [[2026-11-16-003-five-eu-countries-announce-migrant-deportation-centre-in]]
- **TL;DR**: Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands have agreed to establish a 'return hub' in Rwanda for failed asylum seekers, with transfers expected to begin in 2027. The pilot would initially host about 100 single men who have exhausted legal avenues to remain, offering integration in Rwanda or eventual return home. The plan revives the offshore model abandoned by the UK and faces immediate human-rights scrutiny.
