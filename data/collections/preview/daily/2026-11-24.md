## US to blacklist Asian AI firm Megaspeed after Nvidia scandal

- **Type**: digest · **Topic**: world · **Region**: global
- **Source**: [feeds.bbci.co.uk](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cv98xpg5dg5zo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
- **Note**: [[2026-11-24-001-us-to-blacklist-asian-ai-firm-megaspeed-after-nvidia-scandal]]
- **TL;DR**: The US Department of Commerce moved to add Singapore‑based Megaspeed Ltd to its Entity List, accusing the firm of funneling Nvidia AI chips to China via a former executive with alleged espionage ties. The decision heightens geopolitical friction over AI technology, raises questions about export‑control enforcement, and could reshape the global AI supply chain.
