# Daily Hub — 2026-11-28

## Turkish officials 'destroyed' pro-opposition TV channel, owner tells BBC

- **Type**: digest · **Topic**: world · **Region**: global
- **Source**: [feeds.bbci.co.uk](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq36gk151xkdo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)
- **Note**: [[2026-11-28-002-turkish-officials-destroyed-pro-opposition-tv-channel]]
- **TL;DR**: Turkish authorities raided and seized control of Halk TV, a leading pro‑opposition news channel, arresting nine staff members including the owner’s relatives on charges of money laundering, bribery and spreading false information. The move follows a pattern of media takeovers after President Erdoğan’s 2023 re‑election and the recent seizure of another opposition outlet, Sozcu TV.
