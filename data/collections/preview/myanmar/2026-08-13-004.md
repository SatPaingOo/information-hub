# ‘One Rohingya from every household’: Myanmar junta formalises forced conscription in Rakhine State

> [!summary] TL;DR — The Myanmar junta has formalised a policy of forced conscription in Rakhine State, requiring Rohingya communities to provide recruits on a rotating basis. This move has already forced thousands of Rohingya into service, according to residents. The policy is part of the junta's efforts to consolidate power and suppress the Rohingya minority.

## Background

The Rohingya are a Muslim minority group that has faced decades of persecution and violence in Myanmar. The Myanmar military has a long history of human rights abuses against the Rohingya, including forced conscription, rape, and murder. The current junta, which seized power in a coup in 2021, has continued these abuses and has also implemented policies aimed at suppressing the Rohingya minority.

## Analysis: Forced Conscription and Human Rights Abuses

The forced conscription of Rohingya civilians is a clear violation of international human rights law. The policy is part of a broader pattern of human rights abuses committed by the Myanmar military against the Rohingya minority. The international community has consistently condemned these abuses and has called on the Myanmar government to take action to protect the rights of the Rohingya. The formalisation of this policy by the junta is a further escalation of the crisis and highlights the need for urgent action to protect the Rohingya from these abuses.

## Analysis: Regional Implications

The forced conscription of Rohingya civilians has significant regional implications. The policy is likely to exacerbate tensions between Myanmar and its neighbours, particularly Bangladesh, which has taken in hundreds of thousands of Rohingya refugees. The policy may also have implications for regional security, as it could lead to an increase in armed resistance among the Rohingya. The international community should take a strong stance against this policy and work to support the Rohingya in their efforts to protect their rights.

## Analysis: Economic Implications

The forced conscription of Rohingya civilians also has significant economic implications. The policy is likely to lead to a decline in economic activity in Rakhine State, as Rohingya civilians are forced to leave their homes and livelihoods behind. This could have a negative impact on the local economy and may also have broader regional implications, as it could lead to a decline in trade and investment in the region. The international community should take a strong stance against this policy and work to support the Rohingya in their efforts to protect their economic rights.
